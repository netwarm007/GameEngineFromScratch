## Boundary and focused regression

SceneLighting prepares light constants from an explicitly supplied scene and
projection convention. GraphicsManager now supplies the same frame scene
snapshot used by camera preparation, rather than looking the lighting scene up
through Application. The headless test links only SceneLighting, SceneGraph,
Common, and scene identity dependencies, without Manager, Application
implementation, RHI, ImGui, asset IO, or decoders.

The existing shader light layout remains shared through cbuffer.h. Shadow-map
allocation and index assignment remain in the rendering passes.
This checkpoint does not change those resource responsibilities.

The independent lighting regression checks:

- Omni, spot, area, and directional light data and right-handed view transforms.
- Light identity, color, intensity, shadow flag, and attenuation layout.
- Runtime translation and rotation without translation affecting direction.
- OpenGL [-1, 1] versus D3D/Metal [0, 1] shadow depth endpoints.
- Omni square 90-degree projection, spot outer-cone FOV, and area identity
  projection and dimensions.
- Directional fallback placement, camera-relative target, camera clipping
  distances, and the 800-unit orthographic half-extent cap.
- Replacement scenes and light types without retaining previous light data.
- Exactly MAX_LIGHTS live entries, expired weak nodes, rejected overflow,
  unresolved light/camera references, and invalid shadow projection parameters.
  Failed preparation preserves the previously published lighting.

Temporarily selecting D3D/Metal projections for OpenGL caused four depth-endpoint
assertions to fail across omni, spot, and directional cases. The production
selection was restored, the applications rebuilt, and the regression passed.

## Limitations

This checkpoint extracts existing light preparation rather than redesigning
lighting or shadow algorithms. It rejects invalid projection inputs but does
not validate every malformed light transform or attenuation curve.
RHI ownership, platform window adaptation, and ImGui lifecycle remain in their
existing layers. No scheduler or shader changes are included.

Windows OpenGL, Android, WebAssembly, Vulkan, CUDA, and other unlisted
configurations are not verified by this pipeline. Screenshots and the fixed shot
do not establish full rendering or physics correctness.
This record verifies only the source revision above, not a subsequent merge or
the documentation-only commit adding this file.
