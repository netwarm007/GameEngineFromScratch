Each job's exact source revision and successful result were checked. This
diagnostic scene has the same stationary cube and zero-intensity auxiliary spot
light as the [shadow-off run](2c5a40eb05fe1db2f5f2ebc599aa62d583281ad9.md);
the only scene change is enabling the original Lamp shadow. The intervening
change adds that run's documentation.

The three `billiard-screenshots/billiard-after.png` artifacts were inspected
and compared with the corresponding shadow-off artifacts:

- Linux: the 720 by 490 scene viewport is pixel-identical. The Billiard log
  reports that `shadowmap_omni.geom.glsl` could not be opened. This capture
  used Mesa llvmpipe, not hardware OpenGL.
- macOS: the cloth and floor become broadly darker, not just beneath the
  probe. Mean green in the cloth control rectangle `[700,330,750,380)` changes
  from 188.45 to 165.52. This does not demonstrate a correct local shadow.
- Windows: a localized dark silhouette appears on the cloth in the projected
  direction of the probe. Mean green in `[660,284,690,300)` changes from
  184.24 to 159.66, whereas the control `[700,330,750,360)` is unchanged at
  188.59. The existing hover magnifier covers part of the screenshot, not this
  probe shadow. This confirms a visible point-light shadow for this scene,
  not correctness for every cube face or scene.

The averages are display-encoded PNG values, not linear light. The probe's
approximate projected shadow was calculated from the Lamp and cube positions
and cloth height; screenshots alone do not identify which shader or resource
defect causes the Metal or Linux behavior. Viewer artifacts were not inspected
for this diagnostic. Other backends and disabled Android were not tested.

This record applies only to the tested diagnostic revision and does not
verify a merge revision or a rendering fix.
