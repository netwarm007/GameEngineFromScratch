## Scope

From the [memory allocator roadmap](../architecture/memory-allocator-roadmap.md):
`TrackingAllocator`. Tests cover statistics and peaks, zero/overflow/exhausted
requests, rejection of double and foreign frees without reaching the wrapped
allocator, rear guard-band overrun detection (on `Free` and `CheckGuards`),
tagged leak lists and reports, the destructor leak handler, `FreeAll`, and a
four-thread stress test over a TLSF allocator. They also ran locally under
ASan/UBSan (debug and optimized) and TSan on Linux.

## Limitations

- The allocators are not used by other engine modules yet; no rendering
  backend or screenshot behavior was exercised or changed.
- Rear guard only (no underrun detection), no call stacks, no aligned
  allocate; uses the C++ heap and a global mutex, so it is for debug and
  tooling use.
- Fill patterns are still checked only in builds defining `_DEBUG`.
