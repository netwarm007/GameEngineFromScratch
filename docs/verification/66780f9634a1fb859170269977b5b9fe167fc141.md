## Scope

This revision has the same engine sources as the M8.5 revision
(`f72e0d212dc96f55e92887ba007273be40fdc3df`) plus the manual
`Test/Memory/AllocatorBenchmark.cpp` (not built by CMake) and documentation.
It carries the M8.6 decision recorded in the
[roadmap](../architecture/memory-allocator-roadmap.md): the switch stays OFF by
default, with the Linux ON/OFF screenshot comparison and the allocator
micro-benchmark results.

## Tested configurations

- Linux and Windows: `-DUSE_ENGINE_ALLOCATORS=ON -DENABLE_ALLOCATOR_TRACKING=ON`.
- macOS: switch OFF (default).

## Limitations

- The ON/OFF screenshot comparison used different revisions (see roadmap);
  no same-revision OFF baseline, and no Windows or macOS comparison.
- Benchmarks ran locally on 2 vCPUs; no engine-level frame-time comparison.
- The ON configuration is not verified on macOS.
