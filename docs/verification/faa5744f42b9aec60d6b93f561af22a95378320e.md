## Scope

`LinearAllocator` and the marker-based `StackAllocator` (replacing the former
unimplemented shell) from the
[memory allocator roadmap](../architecture/memory-allocator-roadmap.md):
configuration and failure handling, per-allocation alignment, exhaustion and
overflow, reset/reuse, LIFO pop, out-of-order and foreign free rejection,
marker rewind, and randomized push/pop/rewind stress. The tests also ran
locally under ASan/UBSan on Linux with debug and optimized flags.

## Limitations

- The allocators are not used by other engine modules yet; no rendering
  backend or screenshot behavior was exercised or changed.
- Both allocators are single-threaded with fixed capacity.
- A `StackAllocator::Marker` is only meaningful while the stack has not been
  unwound below it; stale markers are not detected.
- Debug fill patterns are checked only in builds defining `_DEBUG`.
