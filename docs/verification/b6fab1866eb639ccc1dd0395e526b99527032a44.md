## Scope

From the [memory allocator roadmap](../architecture/memory-allocator-roadmap.md):
`SlabAllocator` (self-aligned slabs, constructed-object cache, `Trim`) and
`SizeClassAllocator` (16 classes, 16 to 512 bytes). Tests cover configuration
rejection, alignment, object integrity, slab reuse and release, invalid and
double frees (with and without validation), out-of-memory recovery,
constructor/destructor accounting, size-class mapping and waste bounds, and
randomized allocate/free/trim stress. They also ran locally under ASan/UBSan
on Linux with debug and optimized flags.

## Limitations

- The allocators are not used by other engine modules yet; no rendering
  backend or screenshot behavior was exercised or changed.
- Both are single-threaded; without validation, freeing a pointer outside any
  slab is undefined.
- `SizeClassAllocator` rejects requests above 512 bytes and alignment above
  16 bytes.
- Debug fill patterns are checked only in builds defining `_DEBUG`.
