## Artifact observations

Viewer and Billiard screenshots from all three jobs were inspected.

- The scene graph, Viewer, and debug panels are docked, visible, and do not
  overlap or clip. The Viewer panel contains the rendered scene on every
  platform.
- Viewer shows the textured brick plane with "Game Engine From Scratch" text
  and a sphere. Billiard shows the table, balls, and cues.
- The Windows screenshots include a texture-hover tooltip with UV (0.27, 0.60)
  to (0.32, 0.66) and a yellow selection square; this is mouse-hover UI, not an
  overlap of the panels. Its frame rate was low (3.9 and 1.6 FPS).
- The shadow-map preview thumbnails differ between platforms (red on Linux
  and Windows, white on macOS). This was not investigated.
- Linux rendered with Mesa llvmpipe (software OpenGL 4.5).
- Windows archived non-empty `viewer-error.log` and `billiard-error.log`;
  their contents were not analyzed and the jobs still passed.
- Linux produced a coverage summary and HTML report.

## ccache and timing

- Linux and macOS restored the ccache cache saved by the earlier quick jobs
  (4503 and 4504) under the same key (33 MiB and 36 MiB). Build Engine took
  8 s on both. Only restore/save were checked; ccache hit-rate statistics are
  not in the job logs, so no hit rate is claimed.
- The restored source cache is 2.1 GiB; its extraction took 17 s on Linux,
  25 s on Windows, and 46 s on macOS.
- Windows spent 138 s installing tools and 39 s on the ISPC/math regression.
  Windows has no ccache in this change.

## Limitations

- Only this one full run was observed; no cold-cache full run or timing
  comparison against the previous pipeline was made.
- Android is disabled and was not tested.
- Backends tested: D3D12 (Windows), OpenGL (Linux), and Metal (macOS). Vulkan
  was not tested; its dependencies were not found in the Linux and macOS logs.
