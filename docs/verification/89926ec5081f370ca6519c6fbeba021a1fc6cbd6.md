## History of this revision

The revision `89926ec` differs from `47b5b09` only by an empty commit. The
Windows job for `47b5b09` failed before building because Chocolatey returned a
504 while installing CMake; the other two platforms passed it. The empty commit
re-ran all three platforms. Earlier Windows runs also exposed a
`PmrAdapterTest` crash (containers outliving `FreeAll()` in debug builds) and
address-dependent alignment assertions in the Ring, Stack, and PMR tests; both
are fixed in this revision.

## Scope

From the [memory allocator roadmap](../architecture/memory-allocator-roadmap.md):
`FrameAllocator`, `RingAllocator`, the `std::pmr` `PmrAdapter`, and serial-based
stale-marker detection in `StackAllocator`. The tests also ran locally under
ASan/UBSan on Linux with debug and optimized flags.

## Limitations

- The allocators are not used by other engine modules yet; no rendering
  backend or screenshot behavior was exercised or changed.
- Frame and ring allocators are single-threaded with fixed capacity; ring
  fence tracking is the caller's responsibility.
- Debug fill patterns are checked only in builds defining `_DEBUG`.
