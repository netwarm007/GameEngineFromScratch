## Splash checks

The configured suites passed `TEST_PhysicsSceneIntegrationTest`,
`TEST_SceneServicesTest`, and `TEST_OgexParserTest` on all three platforms.
The physics integration test covers the actual Splash asset and Bullet backend:

- The text visibly changes its rendered transform during the introduction.
  The ball remains hidden at its authored starting position, and neither the
  text nor the ball has a physics body yet.
- At 30, 60, and 144 FPS animation sampling, the ball remains hidden immediately
  before the last key. The last key creates both bodies and reveals the ball
  at its starting position, with the text settled into its original flat pose.
- The physics-driven ball reaches the right end within 25 simulated seconds,
  rebounds at least eight times, does not drop into word gaps, and stays within
  0.001 scene units of its authored vertical plane.
- Subsequent sequence updates do not respawn the ball. Reset, reload, skipping
  past the final key, and leaving Splash are checked.
- The original static-text traversal remains a separate physics regression.
  Both physics managers are checked for respecting disabled body creation.

## Scope and limitations

- CI screenshots show individual post-handoff instants, not a recording of the
  entire introduction or proof of every collision. The deterministic phase and
  traversal assertions provide the automated behavioral evidence.
- Animation uses elapsed time while Bullet advances a fixed step per tick.
  Render rate can therefore change the wall-clock pace of physical traversal;
  equal wall-clock ball positions across platforms were not verified.
- The trajectory depends on the authored mesh, initial conditions, material
  values, and Bullet version. This record does not verify modified assets or
  arbitrary collision configurations.
- Windows CI rendered D3D12, not OpenGL or Vulkan. Local Windows OpenGL and
  Vulkan library compilation succeeded, but this is not runtime or
  cross-platform rendering acceptance for those configurations. Android is
  disabled and outside this gate.
- The GitHub commit-status monitor encountered HTTP 403 with its unauthenticated
  rate allowance exhausted. Final results were verified directly with
  CircleCI's workflow, job metadata, test-step results, and artifacts. This did
  not indicate a CI failure; no DNS settings or TLS verification were changed.

This record verifies only the source revision named above. Its documentation-only
follow-up commit does not establish a newly tested source revision. Later source
changes and merge revisions require their own complete CI results.
