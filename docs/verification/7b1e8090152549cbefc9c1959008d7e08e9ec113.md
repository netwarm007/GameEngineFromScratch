## Scope

From the [memory allocator roadmap](../architecture/memory-allocator-roadmap.md):
`RoutingAllocator` (512 B and smaller to `ThreadCacheAllocator`, larger or
over-aligned requests to a mutex-guarded `TlsfAllocator`, routing on free by
arena ownership) and `TlsfAllocator::Owns`. Tests cover invalid requests, the
512/513 boundary, alignments up to 4096, foreign pointers and large double
frees, fallback to TLSF when the slab source fails, exhaustion and recovery,
`FreeAll`, and a 4-thread stress test with cross-thread frees. They also ran
locally under ASan/UBSan (debug) and TSan and at `-O2` on Linux.

## Limitations

- The facade is not used by any engine module yet (M8.2 onward); no rendering
  behavior or screenshot was exercised or changed.
- Fixed TLSF capacity behind one mutex; double frees of small objects are not
  detected.
