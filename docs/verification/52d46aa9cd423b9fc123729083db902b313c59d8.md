## Artifacts and focused checks

- Viewer captures from Windows, Linux and macOS (`viewer-render.png` and both
  scene-cycle captures) were inspected. The scene-tree icons and main Viewer
  content render; no new panel overlap is visible. The global-shadow-map
  thumbnail strip is partly cut by the bottom edge in the standard capture;
  this PR does not change the layout, and the capture does not establish the
  visibility of that lower strip.
- The Linux Vulkan and macOS MoltenVK Viewer captures were also inspected;
  the scene-tree icons and scene render there as well.
- The focused local `AssetLoaderTest` executable passed, including the
  truncated binary read case. The full local CMake configure was unavailable
  because this environment lacks OpenAL and Bison; the local PNG syntax check
  also lacks `zlib.h`. The full platform test results above provide coverage
  for those changes.

## Limitations

- Android is disabled in CI. WebAssembly, FreeBSD, CUDA and OptiX were not
  tested by this pipeline.
- Screenshot inspection does not validate every malformed-input path or
  establish pixel parity across rendering backends.
