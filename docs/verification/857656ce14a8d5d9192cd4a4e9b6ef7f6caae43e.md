## Scope

From the [memory allocator roadmap](../architecture/memory-allocator-roadmap.md):
`GpuSubAllocator` and `ThreadCacheAllocator` (plus
`SizeClassAllocator::ClassIndexOf`). GPU tests cover configuration, alignment
with leading-gap reuse, size and 2^64 overflow limits, coalescing in several
free orders, best fit, stale/forged/double frees, fence-based deferred frees
(out-of-order fences, immediate release for completed fences), `Reset`, and a
randomized workload that checks ranges never overlap and calls `Check()`.
Thread-cache tests cover batching, cache bounds, cross-thread frees, flush on
thread exit, allocator destruction while threads still hold caches, `FreeAll`
invalidation, foreign pointers, backing exhaustion, and an 8-thread stress test.
They also ran locally under ASan/UBSan (debug and optimized) and TSan on Linux.

## Limitations

- The allocators are not used by other engine modules yet, and
  `GpuSubAllocator` is not bound to any RHI backend; no rendering behavior or
  screenshot was exercised or changed.
- `GpuSubAllocator`: one heap, single-threaded, O(log n) rather than constant
  time.
- `ThreadCacheAllocator`: sizes 1..512 only; double frees are not detected;
  `FreeAll()` must not run concurrently.
