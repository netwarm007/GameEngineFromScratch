## Scope and limitations

The collision boxes approximate the modeled pocket contours. Automated
simulation checks establish the tested cushion and pocket behavior; a static
screenshot alone does not demonstrate a ball falling through every pocket.
Scoring, ball removal, jump shots, high-speed continuous collision detection,
other camera aspect ratios, and exhaustive rendering correctness are not
verified here.

Windows CI tests Billiard with D3D12, not its default OpenGL backend.
The same revision also passed a local Windows OpenGL Billiard regression and
focused parser/physics tests, but that is not additional CI backend coverage.
Disabled Android and other unconfigured platforms are outside this acceptance.

Any later source or merge revision needs its own CI results. Documentation-only
follow-up commits do not replace or extend the revision verified by this record.
