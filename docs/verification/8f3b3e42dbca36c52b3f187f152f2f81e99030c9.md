## Corrected behavior and regression coverage

Shadow layers are assigned during CPU light preparation, before OpenGL and
Metal upload their light buffers. Shadow rendering consumes those assignments
without changing the published indices. Spot and area lights share the planar
pool; point and directional lights use independent bounded pools. Excess
casters retain illumination but disable shadow sampling with a diagnostic.
GPU texture allocation remains the rendering backend's responsibility.

Planar shadow pipelines now use the same caster-face policy across backends.
Metal's existing front-face winding already accounts for the shader vertex Y
flip. The cubemap shadow pipeline's platform policy is unchanged.

`TEST_SceneLightingTest`, `TEST_RenderServicesTest`,
`TEST_GuiSubPassLayoutTest`, and `TEST_ShadowShaderCompatibility` were confirmed
passing on all three platforms. Windows also passed `TEST_D3d12Compute`.
The new regressions cover:

- Distinct shadow layers for colored spot lights before backend upload,
  repeated frame preparation, container rehashing, and caster removal.
- Independent shadow-pool capacity boundaries, the shared spot/area pool,
  expired nodes, and disabling excess shadows without dropping lights.
- A frame-begin upload snapshot matching the layers actually rendered by the
  production shadow pass, without subsequent index mutation.
- Consistent planar caster-face selection in the production pipeline setup.

The allocation and upload regressions failed against the original code before
the fix. A host-side build of the macOS pipeline-policy branch also failed the
planar culling checks before the correction and passed afterward. Native
platform CI results above, not that host-side policy check, establish acceptance.

## Limitations

- This verifies the Splash shadow-index and planar caster-face corrections,
  not universal cross-backend pixel equivalence. A pre-existing Billiard felt
  brightness difference remains: a `[700, 330, 750, 380)` cloth patch has mean
  RGB approximately `(45.16, 165.52, 81.06)` on Metal versus
  `(58.33, 189.19, 101.57)` on Linux OpenGL. Each platform's patch means are
  unchanged from its pipeline 949 artifact.
- Individual screenshots do not prove identical animation or physics timing,
  identical ball trajectories, or every possible shadow configuration.
- Other backends, hardware-GPU Linux rendering, disabled Android, and
  configurations not represented by these jobs are not verified.
- The full local project configuration was blocked by missing OpenAL/Flex
  dependencies. Focused local source-target tests passed; the complete native
  builds and suites were verified in the three CI jobs above.

This record verifies only the source revision named above. Its documentation-only
follow-up does not establish a newly tested source revision. Later source
changes and merge revisions require their own complete CI results.
